• Defending 30 runs in the 20th over is NOT high pressure.
• Being 25/3 in the 5th over chasing 190 IS extreme crisis pressure.

⚖️ 2. Phase-Adjusted Par (xRuns): Raw averages lie. Every delivery is benchmarked against phase-specific run expectations (Powerplay 7.8 vs Death 10.4 rpo) to calculate Runs Added and Runs Saved.

🔬 3. Empirical Bayes Shrinkage & Consistency: Regressing small sample sizes toward population priors to eliminate flukes, scaled by game-to-game positive impact frequency.
